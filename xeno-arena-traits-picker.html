Scoring model (from your spreadsheet, Model tab): each family is rated 1–5 on Damage (raw damage contribution), Utility (control/sustain/mitigation/tempo) and CD Efficiency (how often you can actually use it). Blended Score = 0.35×Damage + 0.40×Utility + 0.25×CD Eff, on a 1.00–5.00 scale. Tiers: S ≥3.30, A ≥2.95, B ≥2.55, C below. This is a hypothesis to test against real pets, not measured in-game damage — and it isn't an official Hello Games ranking.
