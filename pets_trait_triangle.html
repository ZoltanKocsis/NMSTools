Companion trait triangle

Each trait owns one corner and the side opposite it. At the corner the trait is 100%; on the opposite side it is 0%, which means its opposite pole is 100%. The dot shows the blend, and the background colour under it is the real mix of the three.

The dot shows proportions only. A 100/100/100 pet and a 10/10/10 pet both sit dead centre — read the exact numbers from the sliders.
